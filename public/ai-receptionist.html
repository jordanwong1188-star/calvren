COMMON QUESTIONS

How the setup works

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Often, yes. Depending on your existing phone provider, calls can be forwarded to a compatible AI phone service. We confirm the available routing options and test them before activation.

Will the receptionist automatically book appointments?

It can gather appointment requests and guide callers through an agreed booking process. Direct calendar bookings depend on the tools you use and an approved, tested integration.

What happens when someone needs a person?

During setup we agree when callers should be transferred or offered a callback, and which team members should receive important enquiries.

Does subscribing activate the phone service immediately?

No. The subscription starts billing when purchased. Phone answering starts only after onboarding, provider configuration and successful call testing. Contact us first if you want to confirm compatibility.
