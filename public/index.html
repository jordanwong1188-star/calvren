A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Clear from
the beginning.

What happens when I try the full demo?

Enter your email and click Open the demo on the page. Submit a fake lead, answer qualifying questions and select a simulated appointment. The simulation uses rules and templates; it sends no customer messages and makes no real bookings. You can separately choose a one-time promotional introduction by email. Live client automation uses OpenAI and connected providers after setup and testing.

Can this work with our existing tools?

We start by checking the tools you use and the access they support. Email, forms, calendars and CRM connections are scoped for each project; the public example demonstrates the workflow before those connections are added.

What runs automatically, and when does a person take over?

We agree the rules before launch. A configured live lead system can respond, ask questions, follow up and book through connected providers. Requests for a person, unsupported requests and uncertain situations pause the conversation for human review. The separate enquiry inbox keeps its drafts for review.

Who is Calvren a good fit for?

Businesses with repeated enquiries, clear qualifying questions and a useful next step such as a viewing, consultation or service appointment. Jordan will check your lead volume, tools and booking rules before proposing a setup.

How is a project priced?

Scope comes first. The workflow, integrations, review requirements and ongoing support determine the proposal. AI provider and third-party subscription costs are discussed separately.
