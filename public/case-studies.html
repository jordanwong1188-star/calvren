ShinerBros Window Cleaning
From a quote request to a clear plan.

Business owner: Jacob. Window-cleaning enquiries often start with just an address and a request for a quote. This example shows a Calvren workflow that could collect job details for Jacob to review, instead of promising a price or appointment before the work has been assessed.

EXAMPLE ENQUIRY · BEFORE

“Can you clean my windows on Friday?” doesn't say how many panes need cleaning, whether access is safe, how many storeys the property has or where it is. Jacob would need another conversation to understand the job.

PROPOSED CALVREN EXPERIENCE

The customer receives a prompt acknowledgement and is asked one useful question at a time. Jacob gets a structured summary covering the property, approximate window count, interior or exterior service, access and preferred day.

How the example workflow would run

  1. 01Identify interior, exterior or combined window cleaning and the property's neighbourhood.
  2. 02Ask about number of storeys, estimated windows, screens, hard-to-reach areas and parking or access constraints.
  3. 03Check whether the request is inside ShinerBros' confirmed service area; escalate safety concerns or uncertain scope.
  4. 04For a Friday request, show Friday consultation or assessment slots only when genuinely available in the configured calendar.
  5. 05Send Jacob a review-ready lead summary. Keep all final quotes, service scope and on-site safety decisions with the business.
◎
Where Jacob stays in control

Jacob would confirm packages, pricing, service areas, available hours and how challenging exterior access is assessed. The example never assumes Calvren can independently quote or guarantee the cleaning job.

ILLUSTRATIVE LEAD RECORD

Business: ShinerBros Window Cleaning · Owner: Jacob
Enquiry: exterior windows · Two-storey home
Customer preference: Friday · Quote: requires review
Next step: consultation or human follow-up · No real booking made

What to measure in a pilot

  • Minutes from new enquiry to first acknowledgement
  • Percentage of quote requests with complete access and property details
  • Number of qualified assessments scheduled and attended
  • Time Jacob spends clarifying missing information

ClearCraft Automotive Detailing
Faster intake. Better booking preparation.

Business owner: Dylan. Detailing customers may message while Dylan is busy with another vehicle. This example shows how Calvren could ask the right questions, qualify the requested service and organize the next appointment discussion without making up prices or confirmed openings.

EXAMPLE ENQUIRY · BEFORE

“How much to detail my SUV this weekend?” doesn't explain the desired package, vehicle size, interior condition, pet hair, stains, or whether the customer prefers mobile service or drop-off.

PROPOSED CALVREN EXPERIENCE

An immediate demo reply asks about the vehicle, package, condition and location. Dylan receives the details together, with special-condition requests flagged for manual review before an estimate or appointment is confirmed.

How the example workflow would run

  1. 01Ask whether the customer wants interior detailing, exterior detailing or a combined package.
  2. 02Collect make and model, vehicle category, visible condition, heavy soiling or pet hair, and service location.
  3. 03Identify requests needing Dylan's review, such as stain removal, paint correction, specialist coatings or uncertain pricing.
  4. 04Ask for a preferred weekday and offer only matching, available demonstration slots or a quote conversation.
  5. 05Summarize the enquiry for Dylan and track the next step without sending real messages or creating bookings in this demo.
◎
Where Dylan stays in control

Dylan would approve service packages, duration, pricing, location, specialist treatments and live calendar rules. A simulated appointment does not represent a real booking with ClearCraft.

ILLUSTRATIVE LEAD RECORD

Business: ClearCraft Automotive Detailing · Owner: Dylan
Enquiry: SUV interior deep clean · Condition: pet hair
Customer preference: Saturday · Pricing: manual review
Next step: assess quote or simulated slot · No real booking made

What to measure in a pilot

  • First-response time when the team is working on vehicles
  • Detailing leads with complete vehicle and condition information
  • Qualified enquiries that become appointments and completed services
  • Changes in manual follow-up time and customer opt-outs

Illustrative only. This proposed flow uses the business information supplied for ClearCraft. It does not claim an installed Calvren system, an endorsement by Dylan, increased revenue or verified conversion improvement.
