A viewing request.
An agent-ready next step.

An agent receives a viewing enquiry with a budget and a rough timeframe. The property reference, contact details and preferred times still need to be collected.

THE STARTING POINT

A message in the inbox. Useful details scattered through the text. The agent starts from scratch.

THE PREPARED OUTPUT

A structured summary, a short list of missing details and a reply draft ready for review.

How the workflow is designed

  1. 01Summarise the property interest and enquiry type.
  2. 02Flag the missing details an agent needs next.
  3. 03Prepare a helpful reply for the agent to review.
◎
Where your team stays in control

The agent checks the property, availability and wording before confirming any viewing.

What a real pilot should measure

  • Time to prepare the first reply
  • Enquiries with complete viewing details
  • Agent time spent on repeat questions

A quote enquiry.
A clearer scope of work.

A service business gets quote requests that leave out the address, the work involved or a suitable time to talk. The team repeats the same questions before it can assess the job.

THE STARTING POINT

A quote request without enough detail. Several replies are needed to understand the job.

THE PREPARED OUTPUT

The request is organised around the service, location and information needed before quoting.

How the workflow is designed

  1. 01Identify the service requested from the enquiry.
  2. 02Prepare a checklist of missing job details.
  3. 03Draft the next reply without inventing prices or availability.
◎
Where your team stays in control

The business reviews the work, decides whether it is a fit and prepares its own quote.

EXAMPLE RECORD

Status: awaiting job details
Next step: collect the service address and a description of the work.

What a real pilot should measure

  • Time spent gathering job details
  • Quote requests ready for assessment
  • Enquiries needing another clarification

Illustrative case study · not a verified client result

Shine Bros Car Detailing
Less chasing. A clearer booking.

Imagine a detailing team finishing a job when a new enquiry arrives. In this illustrative Shine Bros scenario, Calvren starts the conversation and collects the details needed to assess the appointment, while the team stays focused on the vehicle in front of them.

THE SCENARIO BEFORE AUTOMATION

The customer asks for a price and a weekend slot. The enquiry is missing the vehicle type, condition, service location and preferred time. Someone has to pick up the conversation between jobs.

THE PROPOSED CALVREN WORKFLOW

A configured assistant acknowledges the enquiry, asks the next useful question and keeps a lead record. Once the details are complete, it can offer configured calendar slots or pass the job to the team for a quote.

From enquiry to the next appointment

  1. 01Ask which detailing service the customer needs and collect the vehicle type.
  2. 02Clarify interior condition, service location and preferred timing using the business’s questions.
  3. 03Flag unusual damage, specialist treatments or pricing questions for the team instead of guessing.
  4. 04Offer available appointment slots only when the booking rules allow it; otherwise arrange a quote consultation.
  5. 05Confirm the chosen appointment, record the conversation and notify the business. Follow up within the agreed limits if the customer goes quiet.
◎
Where the business stays in control

The owner defines the actual packages, service area, durations and booking rules. Heavy soiling, specialist treatments and any price commitment can require a person’s review. A request for a person pauses automated replies.

SIMULATED LEAD RECORD

Service: interior detailing · Vehicle: SUV
Timing: weekend · Qualification: details collected
Next step: eligible booking slot or team quote review
Delivery: simulation only; no customer message sent

How a real pilot would prove the value

  • Median time to the first response during busy periods
  • How many enquiries contain the details needed to assess a job
  • Qualified enquiries that reach an appointment, and appointments that are attended
  • Team time spent asking repeat questions, plus handoff and opt-out rates

This is a proposed scenario using the business name, not a claim of an installed system, an endorsement or achieved results. Services and operating rules would be confirmed with the owner. No customer revenue, time savings or conversion figures have been verified.

Clear Craft Window Cleaning
A quote request with the details ready.

In this illustrative Clear Craft scenario, a homeowner sends a short request while the crew is working. The proposed Calvren workflow collects the job details first, so a person can review a useful summary instead of starting another round of basic questions.

THE SCENARIO BEFORE AUTOMATION

A short message gives the team little to quote from. The address, number of windows, access and preferred date still need to be discussed. A potential customer may stop replying before the scope is clear.

THE PROPOSED CALVREN WORKFLOW

The assistant asks configured questions about the property, location, window count and access. It keeps the answers together, offers a quote consultation when appropriate and hands unusual jobs to the team.

From enquiry to the next appointment

  1. 01Confirm the requested service, such as exterior window cleaning, against the business’s configured services.
  2. 02Collect the service address or area, property height, approximate window count and access details.
  3. 03Check service-area and booking rules, and send uncertain or unusual access requirements to a person.
  4. 04Offer an available quote-assessment slot if permitted; do not turn an unreviewed enquiry into a confirmed cleaning job.
  5. 05Record the appointment, notify the business and stop follow-ups after booking, opt-out or human handoff.
◎
Where the business stays in control

The team approves the scope, access requirements and final quote. The assistant does not invent prices, promise that a difficult access job is safe or confirm availability outside the connected calendar.

SIMULATED LEAD RECORD

Request: exterior windows · Property: two-storey house
Qualification: job details ready for assessment
Appointment: simulated quote consultation
Final cleaning scope and price: team review required

How a real pilot would prove the value

  • Enquiries with complete location, scope and access information
  • Time from enquiry to an arranged quote assessment
  • Assessments that become accepted quotes, separately from bookings
  • Customer opt-outs, missed appointments and cases needing human review

This is a proposed scenario using the business name, not a claim of an installed system, an endorsement or achieved results. Services and operating rules would be confirmed with the owner. No customer revenue, time savings or conversion figures have been verified.
