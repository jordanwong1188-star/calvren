Privacy,
in plain language.

Updated 2 October 2026.

The public example

The interactive example processes your message in your browser using rules and templates. Downloading an example record saves a file on your device.

Enquiries

When you use a contact form, your name, email, message, consent and any optional business or industry details are stored through Netlify Forms so Calvren can review and respond. The form records the page or workflow that led to your enquiry to help us understand which information is useful.

Client feedback

The client insight form stores your name, company, email, role if supplied, the workflow, your experience, any results you share and your permission choices through Netlify Forms. Feedback is reviewed privately. Written feedback and the identity you permit us to use are considered for publication only after verification and permission checks. Reviewer email addresses are kept private.

Your publication choice

Permission to publish client feedback is optional and separate from consent to review your submission. You can ask to change or withdraw publication permission by emailing Jordan. Submitting a review does not create an automatic public listing.

The protected AI workflow

The private inbox is for authorised users. When a draft is generated, the contact name, business type and enquiry are sent to the configured OpenAI service. The lead and draft are stored in Netlify Blobs. A reviewed draft is a saved record; the starter workflow does not send emails, book appointments or write to an external CRM.

Hosting and fonts

Netlify hosts the website and may process technical information needed to deliver and protect it. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Calvren has not added advertising trackers or visitor analytics.

Access, corrections and deletion

Contact Jordan Wong at Jordan.wong1177@gmail.com or 236-988-8283 to ask about access, corrections, deletion or review publication permission. Records are retained only as needed for the enquiry or agreed business purpose; archived workflow records still need to be deleted separately by the site owner.

Information to leave out

Please avoid confidential client records in the public enquiry and feedback forms. Each business should agree information handling, access and retention requirements before importing client data into a configured workflow.
