Privacy,
in plain language.

Updated 1 October 2026.

The public example

The interactive example processes your message in your browser using example rules and templates. The example message is not submitted to an AI provider or saved to a customer database. Downloading a record saves a file on your own device.

Enquiry form

If you submit the contact form, your name, email, business name, industry, message and consent are stored through Netlify Forms so the site owner can review and respond. Please avoid confidential client information in the form.

The protected workflow

The private inbox is for authorised users. When a user generates a draft, the contact name, business type and enquiry are sent to the configured OpenAI service for processing, and the lead and generated draft are stored in Netlify Blobs. Users should only enter information they have permission to process.

What approval means

A reviewed draft remains a saved record. The starter workflow does not send emails, make bookings, publish content or write to an external CRM. Those actions require separately configured integrations.

Hosting and fonts

Netlify hosts this website and may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the technical details of those font requests. This site does not add advertising trackers or visitor analytics.

Access, corrections and deletion

Use the contact form to request access, a correction or deletion of an enquiry you submitted. Archived workflow records are retained and must be deleted separately by the site owner when no longer needed.

Before processing client data

Each business needs to agree what information its workflow processes, who can access it and how long it is kept. Those requirements should be set before importing real client records into a configured workflow.
