## Written scope

**Problem:** [Describe the repeated task and its effect on the team.]

**Proposed workflow:** [Input → processing → prepared output → human decision.]

**Deliverables:** [Process map, configured prototype, approved connectors, review rules, operating instructions and handover.]

**Included accounts and actions:** [List each system and the authorised actions.]

**Excluded work:** [List the parts outside this proposal.]

**Acceptance checks:** [Normal input, missing details, duplicate handling, provider outage, human review, permission boundaries, export/deletion and fallback.]

**Pilot:** [Who participates, authorised data, agreed volume, baseline, measure and evaluation period.]

**Commercial agreement:** [Agreed setup fee, ongoing fee if any, payment schedule, delivery milestones, and third-party usage costs.]

**Support:** [Contact, coverage, monitoring responsibilities, change requests and response expectations agreed with the customer.]

**Information handling:** [Owner, permitted information, storage locations, access, retention, deletion and provider requirements.]

**Handover:** [Accounts remain customer-owned where agreed; document operation, fallback and access.]

**Approval:** [Customer and Calvren contacts approve the final scope in writing before connecting accounts.]

## After the pilot

Record what changed, what did not, and the agreed measures. Ask separately for permission to publish an accurate case study or customer review. Publication permission does not affect service delivery.
